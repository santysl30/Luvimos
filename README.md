# ❤️ Luvimos

### Understand. Connect. Protect.

Luvimos is a privacy first AI platform focused on **longitudinal emotional intelligence, conversational AI and safety-aware wellbeing support**.

It combines **Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), emotion analysis, Valence-Arousal-Dominance (VAD) modeling, behavioral pattern detection, temporal analytics, and safety mechanisms** to understand emotional patterns over time rather than treating every interaction as an isolated event.

---

## 🎯 Vision

Luvimos aims to create an intelligent and human centered platform that helps users better understand their emotional wellbeing while providing appropriate, privacy conscious support when concerning patterns or safety signals are detected.

The platform is designed for:

* Children and teenagers with appropriate parental/legal-guardian controls
* Young adults
* Adults
* Older adults
* Parents, guardians, caregivers, and trusted care circle members where authorized

Luvimos is designed around **consent, privacy, safety, transparency, and minimum necessary data access**.

## 🔐 Privacy & Safety

Privacy is a foundational principle of Luvimos.

Luvimos will:

* Use data only through authorized mechanisms
* Implement consent and permission management
* Protect sensitive conversational information
* Support age-aware privacy controls
* Support guardian/care-circle permissions
* Minimize unnecessary data collection
* Provide appropriate data access and deletion controls
* Maintain security and audit mechanisms
* Never bypass platform security or encryption
* Never function as covert surveillance

External communication-platform integrations will use only **official and authorized APIs or mechanisms** where technically and legally available.

---

## 👥 Age-Aware Experience

Luvimos is designed to adapt its experience according to age and user relationship.

| User Group | Primary Experience                                     |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 5–8        | Child-focused + strong guardian involvement            |
| 9–12       | Child-focused + guardian support                       |
| 13–15      | Teen-focused + controlled guardian access              |
| 16–19      | Teen/young-adult experience + age-appropriate autonomy |
| 20–30      | Independent adult                                      |
| 31–39      | Independent adult                                      |
| 40–50      | Independent adult                                      |
| 50+        | Independent adult                                      |

Adults may optionally establish a **Care Circle** consisting of trusted people such as family members, caregivers, or other authorized contacts.
